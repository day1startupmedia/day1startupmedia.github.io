AgriBills is building billing software made for the grain trade

AgriBills automates paddy and rice billing, including suit cutting calculations, deductions and lot tracking, and is expanding to all agri-based grains.

By Day1 Startup Media · 24 Oct 2026 · 2 min read
Share Share WhatsApp X LinkedIn Facebook Instagram Story

In the grain trade, billing is still a big headache. Much of it is done by hand, and normal accounting software does not help much, because it cannot handle the complex maths of the trade. AgriBills is a startup built to fix exactly that.

Made for paddy and rice

AgriBills is purpose-built to automate paddy and rice billing. It handles the tricky maths behind suit cutting calculations, deductions and lot tracking instantly, the parts that usually take traders the most time and cause the most mistakes.

The team says it has cracked the hardest part of agricultural accounting. It is now expanding the platform to support all agri-based grains.

Key features

  1. Paddy suit calculation
  2. Bill-wise profit
  3. Trade register, to track the business end to end
  4. One-tap auto purchase option
  5. Ageing due reports

Why it's worth watching

Agriculture is one of India's biggest sectors, but many traders and mills still run on paper and calculators. Software that speaks the language of the grain trade, instead of forcing traders into general accounting tools, could save hours every day.

AgriBills sums up its goal simply: making grain trading smarter, simpler and more efficient.
