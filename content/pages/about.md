**Day1 Startup Media** is a startup news and stories platform focused on new Indian startups.

We cover the companies most outlets skip: young startups, first launches and local businesses doing something new, from metros to Tier-2 and Tier-3 towns. Our stories explain what a startup does, the problem it solves and who it is for, in simple language.

## What we cover

- **Launch:** new products, apps and services
- **Founder Story:** the people behind the idea and why they started
- **Funding:** fresh rounds raised by early-stage startups
- **Local Business:** new local ventures with a startup mindset

## Where to find us

Alongside this website, Day1 Startup Media publishes short startup-story videos on Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, X, Threads and YouTube.
