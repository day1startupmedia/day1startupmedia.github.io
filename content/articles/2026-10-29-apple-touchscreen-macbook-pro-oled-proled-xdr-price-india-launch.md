**Apple MacBook Pro touchscreen is finally here.** At its "Pro and Tell" event in Los Angeles on **28 October 2026**, Apple launched the **MacBook Pro with PROLED XDR Display**, the first Mac ever with a **touchscreen**. It is the biggest MacBook Pro redesign since 2021, and a big U-turn for Apple, which said no to touchscreen Macs for more than 15 years.

## Apple touchscreen MacBook Pro: key specs

- **Display:** tandem **OLED "PROLED XDR"** touchscreen, 10–120Hz adaptive refresh, up to **2,000 nits** peak brightness, 10,000,000:1 contrast. The touch sensor sits inside the panel to cut lag, and a stronger hinge stops the screen wobbling when you tap it.
- **Dynamic Island:** the notch is gone. A pill-shaped Dynamic Island, like the iPhone's, holds the **12MP Center Stage** camera.
- **Chips:** **M5 Pro** and **M5 Max** (not the new M6), with **24GB to 128GB** RAM and up to **16TB** SSD.
- **Design:** thinner by up to about 1 mm; the 14-inch weighs about **3 pounds (≈1.36 kg)**. The **Apple logo on the lid glows** again, like old MacBooks.
- **Ports:** three Thunderbolt 5, HDMI, SDXC card slot, Wi-Fi 7, Bluetooth 6.0.
- **Sizes and colours:** 14-inch and 16-inch, in Space Black and Silver.

## MacBook Pro touchscreen price (US and India)

- **14-inch:** from **$2,899** (M5 Pro, 24GB RAM, 1TB SSD)
- **16-inch:** from **$3,399**
- **Top configuration:** about **$14,549**
- **Price in India:** reported from **₹3.49 lakh** (Tech Observer). Check Apple India's store for the final price.

## Release date

Pre-orders opened on 28 October. The new MacBook Pro goes on sale on **Friday, 6 November 2026**.

## Why it matters

Windows laptops have had OLED touchscreens for years. Apple now has its own answer, aimed at creators and professionals. It is also expensive: it costs much more than the regular non-touch MacBook Pro, which Apple still sells.
