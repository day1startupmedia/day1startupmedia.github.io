Every home and office collects things it no longer needs: old newspapers, cardboard boxes, broken electronics, furniture nobody uses. Getting rid of them properly is often a hassle. **Scrappo**, a Mumbai-based startup, wants to make it simple.

## Pickup from your door

Scrappo is a **doorstep scrap pickup and disposal** service. People can sell, clear and responsibly dispose of unwanted items without carrying anything anywhere. Scrappo handles the process **from pickup to proper disposal**.

## What it takes

- Newspapers and cardboard
- Metals
- **E-waste**
- Furniture removal and dismantling
- Home and office clearance

## Why it matters

In a busy city like Mumbai, most people do not have the time to find a scrap dealer or arrange a clearance. A simple doorstep service saves that effort.

There is a bigger benefit too. When scrap is collected properly, recyclable materials can **reach the right channels** instead of ending up in a landfill. Scrappo is building a more convenient way for Mumbai to deal with old and unwanted items, while helping recycling along the way.
