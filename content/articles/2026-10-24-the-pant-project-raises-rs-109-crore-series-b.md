A brand that started by selling just trousers online is now going big offline. **The Pant Project**, a premium menswear label, has raised **₹109 crore** in a **Series B** round.

## Who invested

The round was led by **Mirae Asset Venture Investments**. Existing investor **Sorin Investments** also joined, along with family offices and angel investors including Anisha Saraf, B&K Securities, MGA Ventures and B2V Ventures. The company also secured venture debt from **Alteria Capital**.

## The founders and the product

The Pant Project was founded by brothers **Dhruv and Udit Toshniwal**. It began as a digital-first brand focused on fit, fabric and comfort for Indian men. Its products include **Malai Pants** and **Inklock No Fade Black Jeans**. It sells on its own website, on Myntra and Amazon, and in its own stores.

Its investor Sorin says revenue has grown **more than 5x** since the Series A round.

## The big plan

The company wants to grow from **25 exclusive stores to more than 150** over the next two to three years. It will also add jeans, athleisure and casualwear, and invest in retail technology that connects online and in-store shopping.

CEO Dhruv Toshniwal says starting online gave the brand the data, product knowledge and brand value to now go deep offline.

## Why it matters

More Indian D2C brands are learning that online is a great start, but physical stores help them reach many more customers. The Pant Project is a strong example of that shift.
