"The spoon is perfect. Don't redesign it." That is what a senior told **Rahul Agarwal** when he was a student at **NID Ahmedabad** in **2014**. He took it as a challenge, and the result was **Polygons**.

## A spoon that lies flat

Polygons is a **flat spoon that turns into an exact measuring spoon when you pinch it**. According to the company, one Polygons spoon **replaces a conventional six-piece measuring spoon set**.

## 100 times the Kickstarter goal

In **2016**, Polygons went to **Kickstarter** with a goal of just **$10,000**. It raised **$1,022,120 from 36,863 backers**, more than **100 times** what it had asked for.

![Graphic of the Polygons Kickstarter campaign numbers](/images/polygons-flat-measuring-spoon-nid-to-shark-tank-kickstarter.jpg)

*The Kickstarter numbers. Graphic: Day1 Startup Media*

## The hard part came after

The real challenge began after the campaign. Getting the spoon made took **about three years**, **more than 1,000 prototypes** and **seven manufacturers**. According to ThePrint, Rahul also made **16 trips to China** along the way.

## The Shark Tank deal

Polygons then reached **Shark Tank USA**. In the episode that aired on **October 14, 2026** (Season 18, Episode 4), **Lori Greiner** and guest Shark **Mindy Kaling** offered **$350,000 for 8% equity plus 2% advisory**, valuing Polygons at **$3.5 million**.

The idea was simple. Making it real took years.
