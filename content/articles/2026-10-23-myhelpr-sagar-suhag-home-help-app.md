Every Indian household knows this morning. A message arrives: the didi who helps at home is not coming today. Dishes are piled up, the cleaning is pending, and guests may be on their way. Gurugram-based founder **Sagar Suhag** is building **MyHELPR** around exactly this everyday problem.

## Home help, the app way

The idea behind MyHELPR is simple: if food can arrive at the door through an app, why can't household help? The platform brings home services onto one app, so families don't have to scramble for a replacement or call around every time their regular help is unavailable.

## How it works

Using MyHELPR is a four-step flow:

1. Open the app
2. Choose the service you need
3. Book it
4. Pay

Depending on availability, help can arrive in about 10 to 30 minutes.

## What it covers

MyHELPR lists a wide range of household work under one roof:

- Cleaning
- Cooking
- Laundry
- Repairs
- Babysitting
- Elder care
- Car wash

That range matters. Instead of separate contacts for a cook, a cleaner and a repair person, the app aims to be the single place a household goes to when something needs doing.

## Why it's worth watching

Quick-delivery apps have already changed how urban India buys groceries and food. MyHELPR applies that same on-demand thinking to one of the most common pain points at home: help that doesn't turn up when it's needed. It's a simple idea that touches the daily routine of a very large number of homes.

Will MyHELPR become India's next big startup? It is one to keep an eye on.
