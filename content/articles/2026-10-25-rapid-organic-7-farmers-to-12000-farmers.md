It started in **2010 with just 7 farmers**. Today, **Rapid Organic** says it works with **more than 12,000 farmers** through contract farming. That is the journey of an organic company that grew by growing alongside the people who farm for it.

## From 7 farmers to 12,000+

Rapid Organic's story begins in 2010, when it started out with only seven farmers. Over the years that circle kept widening, and the company now works with **12,000+ farmers** under contract farming.

Its own line sums up the idea: *"From 7 Farmers to 12,000+ Farmers — Growing Together, Naturally!"*

## 60+ crops and 15+ years in organic

The company works on **more than 60 organic crops** and brings **over 15 years of experience** in the organic sector.

*Rapid Organic at an expo stall. Photo: Rapid Organic*

## A big name in B2B organic supply

Rapid Organic has built a strong identity in **B2B organic supply**. According to the company, it is a **leading manufacturer and supplier** in the organic space, and it puts its **valuation at ₹150 crore**.

## Recognition from the Agriculture Department

The company has also been honoured by the **Agriculture Department**, receiving a certificate of appreciation (प्रशस्ति पत्र) on stage.

![Rapid Organic receiving a certificate of appreciation from the Agriculture Department on stage](/images/rapid-organic-7-farmers-to-12000-farmers-award.jpg)

*Recognition from the Agriculture Department. Photo: Rapid Organic*

## Organic Cumin Tisane: the B2C brand

After years in B2B, Rapid Organic has stepped into retail with its own consumer brand, **Organic Cumin Tisane**, which the company says is growing in popularity. The product was **launched on stage at a Kisan Mela**.

![Rapid Organic's product range banner](/images/rapid-organic-7-farmers-to-12000-farmers-product-range.jpg)

*Rapid Organic's product range. Photo: Rapid Organic*

## Where to buy

Organic Cumin Tisane is available online on **Amazon, Flipkart and JioMart**.

From seven farmers to a network of more than twelve thousand, and from bulk organic supply to a product on online shelves, Rapid Organic's journey is still, in its own words, about growing together, naturally.
