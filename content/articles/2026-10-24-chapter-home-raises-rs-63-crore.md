**Chapter Home**, a young company building home and kitchen brands, has raised **₹63 crore** through a mix of equity and debt. The round values the company at **nearly ₹1,000 crore**, which is big for a startup founded only in 2025.

## Who invested

The round saw participation from **Bessemer Venture Partners**, **Stride Ventures**, comedian and influencer **Tanmay Bhat**, and some family offices.

## The founders

Chapter Home was started in 2025 by **Nitin Chugh, Akarsh Bharadwaj, Shrey Mittal, Dhiraj Jain and Dheeraj Goyal**. The team was part of the founding and leadership teams at **PharmEasy**, so they have built a large consumer business before.

## What it sells

The company builds separate brands for different home and kitchen needs: bed and table linen, cookware, home décor and small kitchen appliances. It entered the market with **HOKIPO**, an online home-furnishings brand. Chapter Home says it uses AI across the business, from product listings and content to demand forecasting and customer targeting.

## Where the money goes

The funds will go into product development, new categories, technology and brand building. It will also grow its omnichannel network, including physical stores that are being developed now.

Co-founder Nitin Chugh says buyers want better design, trusted quality and a good experience both online and in store, which is why the company is building a focused brand for each category.

## Why it matters

Investors are still backing experienced founders in consumer brands, especially those who mix online selling with offline stores.
