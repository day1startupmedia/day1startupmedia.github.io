Most happy customers never leave a Google review. It is not because they had a bad experience. Writing a review simply takes time, and most people move on. **OrbitQR** is a startup built to close that gap for businesses.

## How it works

OrbitQR gives a business a simple **QR code**. A customer scans it, picks a star rating, and gets ready-made review suggestions written with AI. The suggestions come in several Indian languages, including **Hindi, Gujarati, Marathi and Tamil**, so customers can post in the language they are comfortable with.

## Replies on autopilot

Collecting reviews is only half the job. OrbitQR also helps businesses **reply to Google reviews automatically**, whether the review is positive or negative, so no feedback goes unanswered.

## What it offers

- More Google reviews for the business
- AI-generated review suggestions
- Support for multiple Indian languages
- Automatic replies to customer reviews
- A stronger online reputation

## Why it matters

For a local shop, clinic or restaurant, Google reviews are often the first thing a new customer checks. A tool that makes reviewing take seconds, and keeps every review answered, can make a real difference to how much people trust a business.
