Behind many international holiday packages sold by your local travel agent, there is a platform doing the heavy lifting. **TravClan** is one such platform, and it has raised **$5 million** in funding from **Flipkart** and **August One**, along with debt financing from **Trifecta Capital**.

## What TravClan does

TravClan gives travel businesses a single platform to sell and deliver trips: flights, hotels, activities and international holiday packages. It handles pricing, itinerary building, bookings, payments, customer communication and on-ground support at the destination.

So instead of every travel agent building their own systems and supplier tie-ups, they can plug into TravClan.

## The numbers

According to the company:

- More than **15,000 travel agents** across India use its platform
- It processes more than **$100 million in annual travel sales**
- About **40% of its business** now comes from **Tier II and Tier III cities**

## Where the money goes

TravClan will use the funds to grow its global supply network, improve its technology and AI tools, and strengthen operations in key international destinations.

Co-founder and CEO **Arun Bagaria** says the goal is to make TravClan the infrastructure any travel business can build on, so they don't have to recreate the whole travel stack themselves.

## Why it matters

Indians from smaller cities are travelling abroad more than ever, and many still book through a trusted local agent. Tools that make those agents faster and more competitive are a quiet but big opportunity.
